Innovation

A formula designed for its form, not just the jar.

The same recipe behaves differently in a stick, a sachet, a textile format or an airless. Our lab designs the formula together with the packaging and the way it is applied — so it works on the shelf, not just in the beaker.

01
FormFit — texture matched to applicationViscosity, stability and sensorial feel tuned to the packaging and use: stick, pump, sachet, textile format.
02
ThermoSense — warm / cool effectControlled temperature feel without irritation; dermatological tests as standard.
03
K-beauty EU-readySeoul trends translated into claims compliant with Regulation 1223/2009 and CPNP.
04
Full documentationPIF, safety assessment, CPNP, UKCA — included in every project.
